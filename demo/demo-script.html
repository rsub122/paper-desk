Paper Desk demo script

Before you trust an AI trading strategy, get a second opinion in seconds, and see a record of whether we were right.

Peeked · line 31

The problem

  • AI writes a strategy and backtest in minutes. The results are often not real.
  • Five AI trading methods lost 50–72% of their return after the model's training cutoff (Profit Mirage).
  • The code can peek at future prices, or the plan is just lucky after many tries.
  • People decide by looking at a chart.

What we built

  • Plain-English plan in, one verdict out: Trust, Be careful, Do not trust, with reasons.
  • Claude writes the code. 10 Daytona sandboxes test it on 30 stocks.
  • Arize records every step. The "why" answer comes only from that record.
  • Each verdict is sealed with a date, a hash and an Alpaca paper trade.

How we are different

  • Existing checkers are free tools for coders. Ours is plain English, about 30 seconds.
  • We safely run a stranger's code: no internet, no keys in the sandbox.
  • The explanation is checked against the trace, number by number.
  • The sealed record grows with time. Nobody can fake it afterwards.

Before you go on stage

  1. Do one warm-up run 5 minutes before. Then wait 30 seconds.
  2. Reload the Desk page and reset Plans tested so far with ↺.
  3. Turn Demo: plant a cheating line ON.
  4. Open the Arize project paper-desk in a second tab.
  5. Do not restart the dev server. That loses the run.

If something goes wrong

  • Run is slow: keep talking. The terminals are the visual.
  • Run fails: say "live demo", press Test my plan again.
  • Paper order says "accepted": the market is closed. Say "queued for the open".
  • A judge asks if it is rigged: turn the switch OFF and run. It comes back Trust.
Practice only. Not financial advice. Past results do not predict future results.

PAPER DESK - 3-MINUTE DEMO SCRIPT
Daytona HackSprint x Arize, NYC, Oct 16 2026

Pitch: Before you trust an AI trading strategy, get a second opinion in seconds,
and see a record of whether we were right.

------------------------------------------------------------
THE PROBLEM
- AI writes a trading strategy and backtest in minutes. The results are often not real.
- Five AI trading methods lost 50-72% of their return after the model's training
  cutoff (Profit Mirage study).
- The code can peek at future prices, or the plan is just lucky after many tries.
- People decide by looking at a chart.

WHAT WE BUILT
- Plain-English plan in, one verdict out: Trust, Be careful, or Do not trust, with reasons.
- Claude writes the code. 10 Daytona sandboxes test it on 30 stocks.
- Arize records every step. The "why" answer comes only from that record.
- Each verdict is sealed with a date, a hash, and an Alpaca paper trade.

HOW WE ARE DIFFERENT
- Existing checkers are free tools for coders. Ours is plain English, about 30 seconds.
- We safely run a stranger's code: no internet, no keys in the sandbox.
- The explanation is checked against the trace, number by number.
- The sealed record grows with time. Nobody can fake it afterwards.

------------------------------------------------------------
THE SCRIPT
Rory = drives the laptop and does the reveals
Roman = frames the problem and talks during the waits

[0:00] ROMAN
"AI can write a trading strategy in minutes. The problem is the backtests are
often not real. In one study, five AI trading methods lost 50 to 72 percent of
their return once they were tested after the model's training cutoff. And most
people just look at the chart and trust it."
  >> DASHBOARD: Desk page open, empty. "Demo: plant a cheating line" already ON.

[0:20] RORY
"Paper Desk is a second opinion. You type your plan in plain English, and in
about 30 seconds you get one verdict: Trust, Be careful, or Do not trust, with
plain reasons."
  >> DASHBOARD: Point at the 4-step strip across the top.

[0:30] RORY
"Buy Costco when it drops 5 percent, sell after 10. To be upfront: I've switched
on one planted cheating line. Agents don't cheat on cue, and we need to prove
the checker really works."
  >> DASHBOARD: Click the Costco example, then "Test my plan". Live run tab opens.

[0:45] ROMAN
"Claude just turned that sentence into Python. Now ten Daytona computers start
up. Each one is cut off from the internet with no keys inside, because this is
someone else's code. They test it on 30 stocks in parallel, and every line you
see is real output."
  >> DASHBOARD: Terminals stream. Point at the steps: Claude agent, then Daytona sandboxes.

[1:05] ROMAN
"Each sandbox re-runs the strategy 50 times with the future hidden. If a past
decision changes, the code was reading the future."
  >> DASHBOARD: Wait for red "LEAK" lines in the terminals.

[1:15] RORY  (key moment)
"Three point seven million percent. Too good to be true, and here is why: Do not
trust. It peeked at tomorrow's price on this exact line, and we caught it on 30
of 30 stocks."
  >> DASHBOARD: Scroll up to the verdict. Read the line number off the red stamp
     (it changes every run).

[1:30] ROMAN
"Why did it fail? The answer is written only from the Arize trace of this run.
Then we check every number in it against the trace. If the AI invents a number,
it gets flagged."
  >> DASHBOARD: "Why this verdict?" -> "Ask". Point at the badge "Every number
     matches the trace". Optional: flash the Arize tab.

[1:55] RORY
"Fix it. Claude writes four honest versions of the plan, and all four run at
once on Daytona."
  >> DASHBOARD: "Fix it" tab -> "Run 4 variants". Takes about 20 seconds.

[2:05] ROMAN
"We also count every try. Test enough plans and one looks great by pure luck.
With this much data, about 45 tries is the limit. That counter is on screen."
  >> DASHBOARD: Point at "Plans tested so far".

[2:20] RORY
"The cheating line is gone. Here are the honest versions, with how each did on
data the AI never saw."
  >> DASHBOARD: Point at the results table: Locked column and Verdict column.

[2:30] ROMAN
"Last, we seal the verdict with a date, a fingerprint, and a one-share paper

right, and nobody can edit it afterwards."
  >> DASHBOARD: "Seal & share" tab -> "Seal" -> "Open shareable card".

[2:50] RORY  (close)
"Paper money only, not financial advice. Other tools do these checks for coders.

------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU GO ON STAGE
1. Do one warm-up run 5 minutes before. Then wait 30 seconds.
2. Reload the Desk page and reset "Plans tested so far" with the reset button.
3. Turn "Demo: plant a cheating line" ON.
4. Open the Arize project "paper-desk" in a second tab.
5. Do not restart the dev server. That loses the run.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- Run is slow: keep talking. The terminals are the visual.
- Run fails: say "live demo", press "Test my plan" again.
- Paper order says "accepted": the market is closed. Say "queued for the open".
- A judge asks if it is rigged: turn the switch OFF and run. It comes back Trust.

Practice only. Not financial advice. Past results do not predict future results.
